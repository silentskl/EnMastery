# V0.9.0 R11 — Selectable Vocabulary in Reading and AI Feedback

## Rule
Any learner-facing Reading content and AI-generated feedback should support selecting a word or short phrase and saving it to Vocabulary.

## Covered surfaces
- Reading passage
- Reading questions and options
- Reading answer feedback and explanations
- Reading Past Work
- Writing live AI Review
- Writing Past Work AI Review
- Speaking AI tutor reply, strengths, improvements, suggested expressions
- Speaking pronunciation assessment notes
- Listening answer feedback and explanations
- Intensive Listening dictation and shadowing feedback

## Interaction
1. Select a word or phrase (maximum 12 words).
2. Choose **Explain & save**.
3. Review the generated vocabulary card.
4. Choose **Add to my vocabulary**.
5. The selected context is stored with the vocabulary item for later review.

Third-party media itself is not copied into the vocabulary system; only the learner-selected term/phrase and permitted local lesson context are saved.
