# R19 Hotfix 4 — Curated publisher fallback

Curated stories now resolve in two stages: (1) approved publisher YouTube video when available; (2) the configured official publisher-hosted story page when no eligible embeddable YouTube copy exists. Publisher fallback content remains reference-only and is never copied into English Mastery. Students open the publisher video in a new tab and return to complete listening passes, questions and summary.

This specifically fixes British Council stories such as `story-r9-059` (The animal shelter), which is available on the official LearnEnglish Kids site but may not have an eligible YouTube copy on the configured publisher channel.
