# EnMastery — Singapore PSLE Oral & Listening

EnMastery is a **Primary 5–6 English oral and listening practice platform** for Singapore PSLE preparation. All other subject and exam sections have been removed from the active application.

## Three learning pathways

1. **Stimulus-Based Conversation** — students discuss a photograph, support opinions and personal experiences, hear examiner follow-up questions and receive actionable AI feedback.
2. **Reading Aloud** — students record a passage, then receive pronunciation, fluency, accuracy and expression feedback; Azure Speech supports acoustic assessment when configured.
3. **Listening Comprehension** — students listen to audio, replay difficult sections, and answer grounded comprehension questions. Completing listening questions does not require writing a summary.

**PSLE reference:** Paper 4 Oral Communication contains Reading Aloud (15 marks) and Stimulus-Based Conversation (25 marks). Paper 3 Listening Comprehension contains 20 multiple-choice questions for 20 marks. Short lessons are skills practice rather than complete practice papers. Scores generated by AI are formative and **not official SEAB marks**.

Official source: https://www.seab.gov.sg/psle/psle-formats-examined-in-2026/

## Interactive PSLE picture-conversation protocol

A learner first observes and describes a real picture. The AI examiner then asks **four adaptive questions**, one at a time. The learner must respond to each before the examiner proceeds; the entire transcript and examiner's replies are persisted to the learner's conversation session. Per-turn feedback is private until the final round. The daily conversation achievement updates **only after all four questions have been answered**. Incomplete picture tasks are not assigned, and the administration studio requires a working image URL plus an accurate teacher-side scene description. Sixteen sample P5/P6 photo-backed prompts are seeded in migration 0067; photo delivery is from third-party image hosts and may require replacing URLs if a provider removes an image.

## Everyday AI role-play (separate from PSLE picture talk)

In addition to the picture-first PSLE oral simulator, learners can choose from **105 life situations in 12 categories** at `/oral/roleplay`. These include restaurants and hawker centres, hotel check-in/check-out, MRT and airport journeys, retail customer service, school situations, family communication, public services, health, leisure, technology, outdoor activities and everyday problem-solving. Each scenario defines an AI character, a student role, situation, opening line and optional conversational goals.

Role-play is **open-ended rather than a fixed-question examination**: students can speak or type freely, request clarification, negotiate, correct misunderstandings and end whenever ready. The AI stays in role and adapts its replies to the learner's spoken statements and saved D1 dialogue history. Select Beginner / Everyday / Challenge difficulty. TTS reads the character replies and STT captures answers where configured. A session's language feedback is generated only when the student chooses to end. This experience supplements the PSLE oral modules; it is not an official PSLE paper.

The scenarios are authored in `lib/roleplay/scenarios.ts` and need no additional D1 migrations: authenticated sessions and turns reuse the existing `speaking_sessions` / `speaking_turns` schema, with a `roleplay-` identifier and a validated server-owned scenario ID. Learner messages do not define roles or system prompts, and no transactions or real bookings take place.
