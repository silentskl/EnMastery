Behavioral rules
- Simply opening a study page does not count study time.
- A quiet interval is pending until another valid activity arrives before 60 seconds.
- If 60 continuous seconds pass without valid activity, that whole quiet interval is discarded and exactly one idle episode is added.
- Staying idle does not repeatedly increment the counter.
- After activity resumes, a later independent 60-second idle period increments idle again.
- Valid activity includes mouse/touch/keyboard/text input, supported audio/video/YouTube playback, microphone recording and browser speech recognition.
- Reward-game routes are excluded from study time.
- Daily calendar shows effective study duration plus Idle N×.
